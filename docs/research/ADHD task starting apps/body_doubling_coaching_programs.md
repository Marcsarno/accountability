# Body doubling services, ADHD coaching apps and structured/clinical ADHD programs: mechanics, evidence and what to rebuild

Research date: 2026-10-28. Prices and features change often; each item notes the date of its source where one was available. Most product details come from official sites and help centres. Most prices and ratings come from third-party aggregators, which often disagree with each other. Those cases are flagged.

## 1. Core mechanics: how each product structures starting and finishing a task

### Takeaway
Body doubling products (Focusmate, Flow Club, Dubbii, and the co-working rooms in Shimmer and Inflow) share one short loop: **book or join at a set time, state the task out loud, work alongside someone, check in at the end.** Coaching (Shimmer) wraps a weekly cycle around that loop: plan with a coach, do the steps, text check-ins during the week, reflect. Inflow is a CBT skills course (modules, quizzes, journaling) that also offers body doubling and coaching that replies by message. EndeavorOTC works differently from all of these. It is a daily attention-training video game and does no task planning or accountability.

### Cited Findings
**Focusmate (1:1 video co-working with a stranger or a favourite partner)**
- Sessions last 25, 50 or 75 minutes. "You say hello at the beginning, declare your tasks, and say goodbye at the end." Partners work on their own tasks, then check in on progress at the end. Sessions run 24/7, and "over 99% of bookings get matched with a partner." — [Focusmate FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq)
- Sessions sit on the calendar ("We'll also send you calendar invites"). If no partner is found, you can book the next slot, 15 minutes later. You can star partners as favourites. You can report a partner who goofs off. — [Focusmate FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq)
- If your partner does not show up, you can ask for a rematch. — [Focusmate help: partner doesn't show](https://support.focusmate.com/en/articles/4044431-what-if-i-don-t-get-a-match-or-my-partner-doesn-t-show)
- Third-party descriptions say both partners keep cameras on for the whole session. — [Reddit snapshot / comparison summaries via search](https://mutra.pages.dev/compare/alternatives/focusmate)

**Flow Club (small hosted group sessions)**
- A volunteer member hosts each session. The host opens by asking each person what they are working on, and everyone works quietly together ("body doubling"). Some formats use Pomodoro-style breaks. The session ends by "celebrating incremental progress," and members are asked for feedback after every session. Sessions last 30 minutes to 3 hours. The cap is 9 people, cut to 5 for 30-minute sessions because check-ins took too long. Chat-only, non-verbal sessions exist "for people with limited social energy." — [Flow Club blog: body doubling session format (updated 12 Aug 2026)](https://www.flow.club/blog/body-doubling-session-format)
- Hosts are peers, not coaches. A member can become a host after attending 10 sessions. The help centre claims more than 2,500 sessions a week. — [Flow Club help: How does Flow Club work?](https://help.flow.club/en/articles/782977) (as summarised in search results; a 2026 roundup at [toolfinder.com](https://toolfinder.com/lists/virtual-co-working-clubs) says 1,700+ a week)
- The company says the platform has hosted more than 400,000 body doubling sessions. — [Flow Club blog](https://www.flow.club/blog/body-doubling-session-format)

**Dubbii (ADHD Love, UK; asynchronous body doubling on video)**
- The user follows pre-recorded videos of the creators ("Rich & Rox") doing a task alongside them. The app also runs daily live body-doubling sessions and a weekday "power hour." The task library focuses on the most-requested tasks, mostly household chores and admin. Tasks are split into micro-steps. Progress earns badges. Paid users can create "unlimited nudges" (reminders). — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6450302677); [developer case study (Gravitywell)](https://gravitywell-website.pages.dev/projects/dubbii); [Gravitywell: designing an ADHD-friendly app](https://gravitywell-website.pages.dev/insights/how-we-designed-an-adhd-friendly-mobile-app)

**Shimmer (1:1 ADHD coaching plus community)**
- Members get a recurring weekly 30-minute 1:1 video session with a coach and can text or message the coach between sessions. The first session sets long-term direction, then breaks it into actionable steps. After that, coaching runs in a weekly cycle of trying strategies and reflecting on them, organised around "Time, Done, Pause." The coach uses the member's answers to Shimmer's ADHD assessments. With opt-in, the app summarises "key insights and committed actions" across sessions. Messages in the app prompt the member to pause and celebrate wins. — [shimmer.care homepage (fetched Oct 2026)](https://www.shimmer.care/)
- Daily body-doubling sessions run 2–3 times a day, typically 25–50 minutes with a brief check-in. Members also get weekly 45–60-minute workshops, learning journeys, a "Playbook Library," and "Smart Tools (AI + templates)." — [shimmer.care](https://www.shimmer.care/)
- The App Store listing describes "text-based accountability check-ins throughout the week from your coach." — [Shimmer App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1570668247)
- Shimmer says only 3.7% of coach applicants pass vetting. Listed coach credentials vary (ICF ACC/PCC, some marked "in progress"; NBC-HWC; CACP), and several coaches have no listed coaching credential. — [shimmer.care](https://www.shimmer.care/)

**Inflow (CBT-based self-guided program)**
- Structured modules on executive-function problems include psychoeducation, short quizzes and interactive journaling. The design aims for daily use: practise new behaviours and challenge negative thoughts. The app also has "body doubling" sessions and asynchronous coaching (message a trained coach and get a reply within 48 hours). — [Simply Psychology summary of the 2025 RCT (12 Feb 2026)](https://www.simplypsychology.org/?p=97474)
- Users and reviewers describe live "Co-working" and "Focus Room" sessions. One Trustpilot reviewer says they "have become the basis of my new work routines." — [Trustpilot: getinflow.io](https://at.trustpilot.com/review/getinflow.io); [NeuroLaunch Inflow vs Focusmate](https://neurolaunch.com/inflow-vs-focusmate-adhd/)

**EndeavorOTC (Akili, now part of Virtual Therapeutics; over-the-counter attention game)**
- The game uses tilt steering plus tapping targets (multitasking with interference). In the trial, the dose was **25 min/day, 5 days/week, for 6 weeks**, played at home. The product has no planning, reminders or accountability features. Its target is attention as measured in a lab. — [Maple et al., CNS Spectrums 2024/2025](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cns-spectrums/article/singlearm-pivotal-trial-to-assess-the-efficacy-of-aklt01-a-novel-digital-intervention-for-attention-in-adults-diagnosed-with-attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder/46F4D5CEAF7A8025FC3C9640F96DDB1F); complaints about tilt gameplay and motion sickness are summarised from [app aggregator reviews](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6447322997)
- Akili agreed to sell itself to Virtual Therapeutics for about $34M (deal expected to close Q3 2024). — [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/adhd-game-developer-akili-interactive-be-taken-private-after-being-sold-34m?utm_id=55)

**Theorised mechanisms of body doubling (from the research literature, not tested)**
- Accountability, help starting a task, motivation, fewer distractions, social pressure, external structure, habit cues, emotional regulation, companionship, and less anxiety about a hard task. — [Tan, Hegemann & Gerken, "A Roadmap of Mixed Reality Body Doubling for Adults with ADHD," arXiv May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07851)
- Eagle et al. propose two dimensions: a **space/time** continuum (same room → online → recorded media) and a **mutuality** continuum (ambient companionship → performance/accountability). — [Tan et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07851); [Ara et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1)

### Inferences
- Across the products, the shared loop has four parts: (1) a fixed start time someone else knows about, (2) saying the task out loud, (3) someone else present during the work, (4) an end check where you report what got done. Focusmate is this loop at its smallest.
- In Eagle's model, a family member saying "I'm here too" sits at the ambient-companionship end of the mutuality axis. A family member who asks "did you finish?" at the end moves it toward accountability. The app can let the user choose how much accountability they want.
- Dubbii shows that a recorded or asynchronous "companion" for household tasks is a product people use. A family app could reuse short pre-recorded clips of a family member ("I'm folding laundry too, let's go") for cases where nobody is free live.
- EndeavorOTC's mechanic (a daily game) does not address starting tasks or remembering them, so the PWA has little to copy from it.

### Gaps
- Focusmate's 2026 policy on cameras (required or optional) and its current reminder/notification set were not confirmed from a primary page. The FAQ does not describe push or email reminders beyond calendar invites.
- Shimmer's AI "Smart Tools" are not explained on its site.
- I could not confirm from a primary source whether Inflow's current modules include specific CBT exercises (for example, task chunking or reframing thoughts), or how many modules there are.

## 2. How they get the user to show up (booking, reminders, commitment devices, no-show policies)

### Takeaway
The services rely on **pre-commitment to a specific time slot that another person depends on**, plus reputational cost for no-shows (Focusmate) or social belonging and host roles (Flow Club). Coaching uses a recurring weekly appointment plus a coach who texts. Paid subscriptions and refund conditions also act as commitment devices. Dubbii and Inflow lean on in-app nudges. Showing up is the weakest point for apps the user must remember to open.

### Cited Findings
- **Focusmate timeliness score:** "If you're late or don't show, Focusmate can detect it and your timeliness score will be reduced, and your account can also be frozen." — [Focusmate FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq)
- In a 2018 version of the rules, an account froze after a no-show until the user reactivated it. A no-show counted at 10 minutes late for the freeze and at 5 minutes for the "Attendance Score." These rules may be out of date. — [Focusmate blog, Aug 2018](https://www.focusmate.com/blog/feature-update-august-13-2018-streaks-auto-cancel/)
- Focusmate sends calendar invites for every booked session, and the free plan caps users at 3 sessions a week. The weekly cap creates scarcity and makes booking a deliberate act. — [Focusmate FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq)
- **Flow Club:** sessions are scheduled with a named host, and members can become hosts after 10 sessions, with up to 50% off for hosting. Hosting builds an identity and a role. Subscriptions can no longer be paused. — [search summary of Flow Club help centre](https://help.flow.club/en/categories/189953); [Flow Club hosting guidelines](https://flow.club/hosting-guidelines)
- **Shimmer:** "recurring weekly" 1:1 appointments, the coach texts during the week, and in-app messages prompt the member to celebrate wins. The 30-day money-back guarantee requires attending at least 3 coaching sessions (or 2 body doubling sessions and 1 learning journey for community members), which builds attendance into the trial. — [shimmer.care](https://www.shimmer.care/)
- **Dubbii:** paid users get "unlimited nudges." — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6450302677)
- **Inflow:** the 7-day trial converts to a paid plan automatically, and you must cancel at least 24 hours before renewal through its subscription portal. Deleting the app does not cancel. Several Trustpilot reviewers call this a "trap." — [search summary citing getinflow.io FAQ and Trustpilot](https://at.trustpilot.com/review/getinflow.io)
- **Research on if-then planning:** in small lab studies of **children** with ADHD, if-then plans (implementation intentions) improved delay of gratification (n=45 inpatient; replication n=47 outpatient vs 40 controls), response inhibition (Go/No-Go), and perseverative errors. The highest inhibition came from combining if-then plans with stimulant medication. — [Gawrilow, Gollwitzer & Oettingen 2011 (PDF)](https://www.socmot.uni-konstanz.de/sites/default/files/Gawrilow_Gollwitzer_Oettingen_Delay_2011-2.pdf); [Konstanz lab publication list](https://www.socmot.uni-konstanz.de/node/95)

### Inferences
- For a user who forgets to open apps, the product has to start the session itself rather than wait for the user. The services do this by putting a booked time on the calendar and having a real person waiting at that time.
- A family member is a stronger commitment device than a stranger in one way (the relationship continues) and weaker in another (no score or freeze, and the family member may let things slide). A gentle, visible "showed up" streak shared with the companion can replace Focusmate's timeliness score without punishing the user.
- If-then plans ("When I finish breakfast, I start the laundry session") are cheap to put in an app and have experimental support in children with ADHD. Adult evidence was not found in this search.

### Gaps
- I did not verify Focusmate's current 2026 reminder set (push or email before a session) or the current freeze rule from a primary help article.
- I found no published data on no-show rates for any of these services.
- I found no adult ADHD trial of implementation intentions in this search.

## 3. Evidence: trials, surveys and anecdote

### Takeaway
Evidence is weak to moderate everywhere. **EndeavorOTC** has the most formal evidence (FDA pathway, a 221-adult multicentre trial), but the trial was **single-arm with no control group**, funded and run by the company, and its main outcome is a lab attention test, not real-world starting or finishing of tasks. **Inflow** has one open feasibility study and one waitlist-controlled RCT (N=154). Both report symptom gains, but neither found an effect on functional impairment, and both had company-linked authors. **Body doubling** has a large survey of how people use it (Eagle et al., N=220), one small within-subjects VR study (N=12), and some null theses. No peer-reviewed RCT exists. **Shimmer/Focusmate/Flow Club/Dubbii** cite only internal, undisclosed user stats. **ADHD coaching** in general has mostly uncontrolled studies in college students.

### Cited Findings
**EndeavorOTC / AKL-T01 (adults)**
- Trial: STARS-ADHD-Adult (NCT05183919), multicentre (14 US sites), **single-arm, open-label**. Enrolled adults ≥18 with inattentive or combined ADHD and TOVA ACS ≤ −1.8. 440 screened, **221 enrolled, 153 analysed** (mean age 39.9; 70% female; 39% on stimulants). Dose: 25 min/day, 5 days/week, 6 weeks. — [Maple, Savarese, Stamatis, Mercaldi, Kollins. CNS Spectrums 29(5), published online 10 Jan 2025](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cns-spectrums/article/singlearm-pivotal-trial-to-assess-the-efficacy-of-aklt01-a-novel-digital-intervention-for-attention-in-adults-diagnosed-with-attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder/46F4D5CEAF7A8025FC3C9640F96DDB1F)
- Primary outcome: TOVA Attention Comparison Score rose by a mean of **6.46 (SD 6.95), p<.0001**. Secondary outcomes: ADHD-RS-IV inattention, ADHD-RS-IV total and Adult ADHD Quality of Life all improved (p<.0001; the abstract gives no effect sizes). Exploratory: **36.6%** reached the normal TOVA range, and **27.1%** had a ≥30% ADHD-RS improvement. 5% had adverse events, none serious. Mean compliance was 81.1%. All authors were affiliated with Akili. — [same](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cns-spectrums/article/singlearm-pivotal-trial-to-assess-the-efficacy-of-aklt01-a-novel-digital-intervention-for-attention-in-adults-diagnosed-with-attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder/46F4D5CEAF7A8025FC3C9640F96DDB1F)
- **Conflicting figures:** company and press materials report "83%" (or in marketing "88%") of participants improving on TOVA, **32.7%** with ≥30% ADHD-RS reduction, 72.5% with some quality-of-life improvement, and 45.8% with clinically meaningful QoL improvement. The peer-reviewed responder rate is lower (27.1%). — [Managed Healthcare Executive](https://www.managedhealthcareexecutive.com/view/first-ever-therapeutic-video-game-for-adult-adhd-approved-by-fda); [EndeavorOTC App Store marketing](https://apps.apple.com/US/app/id6447322997); [Maple et al.](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cns-spectrums/article/singlearm-pivotal-trial-to-assess-the-efficacy-of-aklt01-a-novel-digital-intervention-for-attention-in-adults-diagnosed-with-attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder/46F4D5CEAF7A8025FC3C9640F96DDB1F)
- **Regulatory status:** EndeavorOTC launched in June 2023, and an older company FAQ stated that it "has not been authorized by the FDA." That launch appears to have relied on FDA enforcement discretion, but this is inferred from the FAQ wording, not confirmed. Later trade-press reports describe FDA clearance through the **510(k)** pathway, for "adults 18+ with primarily inattentive or combined-type ADHD" to improve attention function, ADHD symptoms and quality of life. — [EndeavorOTC FAQ (older)](https://www.endeavorotc.com/faq/); [Medical Device Developments](https://www.medicaldevice-developments.com/news/akilis-endeavorotc-digital-treatment-gets-fda-clearance-for-adhd/); [MBT Mag](https://www.mbtmag.com/industry-4-0/news/22913014/fda-clears-video-game-for-treating-adults-with-adhd); [Fierce Biotech launch 2023](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/akili-launches-over-counter-version-attention-improving-video-game-adults-adhd)
- A companion paper reports two single-arm trials of AKL-T01 (adolescents and adults), with an adult TOVA ACS change of about 6.5. — [PMC11187123 / npj Mental Health Research 2024](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11187123/)

**Inflow**
- **Open feasibility study:** Knouse et al., PLOS Digital Health, Aug 2022. 240 adults started, followed at 2 weeks (n=114), 4 weeks (n=97) and 7 weeks (n=95), so about 60% were lost by week 7. Usability ratings were favourable, and median use was 3.86 times a week. Most of those who stayed rated their symptoms as less severe. **Uncontrolled.** Inflow co-founders were among the authors (disclosed). — [PMC9931323](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9931323/)
- **RCT:** Antshel, McBride & Knouse, "Bridging the Gap: Digital CBT for Adults Managing ADHD Challenges," *Journal of Attention Disorders* (2025), DOI 10.1177/10870547251384462. **N=154**, ages 18–55, randomised to 8 weeks of the app or a **waitlist**. Significant reductions in inattentive and hyperactive-impulsive symptoms and better ADHD quality of life. **No significant effect on functional impairment.** Participants who completed more exercises had larger reductions. The sample was mostly female, college-educated and self-selected, and most were already on medication or in therapy. The authors say the waitlist design limits confidence. About 8.5% of screened people reported suicidal thoughts. — [SAGE PDF](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/10870547251384462); [Simply Psychology summary, 12 Feb 2026](https://www.simplypsychology.org/?p=97474)
- A secondary source says the conflict-of-interest statement names Inflow Holdings, Inc. (an author is a paid consultant with stock options), and that the abstract does not name the app. — [search summary of secondary source](https://www.simplypsychology.org/?p=97474) (not verified against the full paper)

**Body doubling**
- **Eagle, Baltaxe-Admony & Ringland (2024)**, "'It was something I naturally found worked and heard about later': an investigation of body doubling with neurodivergent participants," *ACM Transactions on Accessible Computing*. A **survey of 220 neurodivergent people** (ADHD, autism, OCD and others) about how, when and why they body double. It reports use cases, activity types, modality, location, who the body double is, and theorised mechanisms. It proposes the space/time × mutuality model (first given in a 2023 poster). **Descriptive survey, no outcome measurement.** — [Tan et al. 2026 review](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07851); [Ara et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1)
- **Ara et al. (preprint, Sept 2025):** **12 adults with ADHD**, within-subjects, a 20-minute VR bricklaying task done alone, with a human double, or with an "AI" double (actually a Wizard-of-Oz researcher). Bricks per minute: 8.49 alone, 10.82 with the human, 11.06 with the AI (F(2,22)=6.51, p=0.006). Self-rated sustained attention also rose (p<0.001). **No difference between human and AI doubles**, and no effect on accuracy. 10 of 12 said working alone led to distraction. Preferences were split: some preferred a human for motivation, others an AI because there was no judgement. — [arXiv 2509.12153](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1)
- **Null or mixed results:** Born (2024) with ADHD participants found no significant effect, although participants found body doubles motivating. Annavarapu (2024) with neurotypical participants found no behavioural differences. Cuber et al. (2024) reported better concentration and motivation in a VR study of 27 students with ADHD. A Drake University poster (2025) using a brain-computer interface found no significant effect on performance, focus or calm. — [Ara et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1); [CCSC Central Plains 2025 poster](https://www.ccsc.org/centralplains/2025/talks/talk-poster16/)
- O'Connell et al. (2024): a robot body double for college students with ADHD. "73% of participants desired attention monitoring and distraction detection features." — [Tan et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07851)
- Ara et al. state that no peer-reviewed controlled trials had examined body doubling in adults with ADHD before their work. Tan et al. (May 2026) call the literature "limited to only a small number of papers and master's theses" and ask for empirical validation. — [Ara et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1); [Tan et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07851)
- Medical News Today notes that body doubling has not been tested in controlled experiments and may not work for everyone. — [Medical News Today](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/body-doubling-adhd)
- A third-party blog claims a "2023 University of Sussex preprint found virtual body doubling measurably reduced task avoidance in adults with ADHD." **Unverified. I could not find the primary source.** — [adhd-productivity.pages.dev (via search)](https://adhd-productivity.pages.dev/blog/best-body-doubling-apps-adhd-2026)

**Company-reported statistics (no method disclosed; treat as marketing)**
- Focusmate: neurodivergent members report "a 161% increase in productivity on average," 33% more than neurotypical members. No sample or method is given. — [Focusmate FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq)
- Shimmer: "83% of members reported better ability to manage their ADHD in 6 weeks." No sample, method or definition is given. — [shimmer.care](https://www.shimmer.care/)
- Dubbii: says in-app body doubling "worked in testing with key users." No independent study. — [Gravitywell case study](https://gravitywell-website.pages.dev/projects/dubbii)

**ADHD coaching in general**
- In Ahmann, Tuttle & Wright's review, 18 of 19 outcome studies found that ADHD coaching supports improvements. The studies were mostly of students, many uncontrolled, and none reported individual coaching for adults. Ahmann & Saviet later describe a manualised 12-session adult protocol and say its effectiveness still needs research. — [Ahmann & Saviet, IJEBCM](https://radar.brookes.ac.uk/radar/items/a4a8d49e-0f1c-4741-b550-82533d403b7e/1); [MUIH 2017 symposium abstracts](https://www.muih.edu/sites/default/files/2017_Research_Symposium_Abstracts%20_Posters.pdf); [ADHD Coaches Organization research list](https://www.adhdcoaches.org/adhd-coaching-research)

### Inferences
- Strength of evidence, strongest first: Inflow RCT (randomised, but waitlist control, self-report and company-linked) > EndeavorOTC (large but uncontrolled; lab-measured attention) > Inflow feasibility (uncontrolled, heavy dropout) > coaching literature (mostly uncontrolled students) > body doubling (survey + N=12 VR + nulls) > company stats (anecdote).
- None of these studies measure "started and finished basic tasks at home" directly. The Inflow RCT's null result on functional impairment suggests symptom gains do not automatically become daily functioning.
- Inflow's dose-response finding (more exercises → bigger gains) and the 60% loss in its feasibility study both point to engagement as the bottleneck. That fits a user who "forgets to open apps."
- The Ara et al. human ≈ AI result, though tiny, suggests even a lightweight "presence" (a scheduled virtual or recorded companion) might help when no family member is available.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the FDA 510(k) number or decision date for EndeavorOTC from the FDA database. The clearance is reported by trade press only.
- I did not read the full text of the Inflow RCT for effect sizes, dropout or the exact conflict statement.
- I did not obtain the full text of Eagle et al. 2024 (breakdown of who the body doubles were, ADHD share of the sample).
- I found no published evidence for Focusmate, Flow Club, Shimmer or Dubbii.

## 4. What ADHD users praise and complain about

### Takeaway
Users praise body doubling for getting them started and for low-pressure company. The main complaints are **anxiety about cameras and being watched** (rejection sensitivity), **price**, and **billing traps**. EndeavorOTC draws complaints about cost, short trials, motion sickness and repetitive gameplay. Direct Reddit access was limited, so the snapshot sources below are secondary.

### Cited Findings
- Camera-on rules and verbal check-ins in many live ADHD body-doubling groups felt uncomfortable to some Reddit users. One proposed a camera-optional Focusmate-style session. Others body-double with friends on Discord with cameras optional. Commenters linked performance anxiety and rejection sensitivity to finding it hard to focus "when all eyes are on you." — [Reddit snapshots (third-party archive)](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1scn98w/snapshots/2026-04-10T20%3A30%3A07.765544Z); [another snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1t4cdj6/snapshots/2026-05-08T19%3A50%3A12.016367Z)
- A third-party comparison says Focusmate's camera requirement creates anxiety for some users. — [mutra.pages.dev](https://mutra.pages.dev/compare/alternatives/focusmate)
- Flow Club built chat-only, non-verbal sessions for people with "limited social energy." This suggests the company heard the same complaint. — [Flow Club blog](https://www.flow.club/blog/body-doubling-session-format)
- A single G2 reviewer says Focusmate works better for them than stimulants. One reviewer, so anecdote only. — [G2 Focusmate](https://www.g2.com/sellers/focusmate)
- Inflow: Trustpilot score about 3.5/5 from about 90 reviews. Praise centres on learning modules and co-working/Focus Rooms. Complaints are about double charges and an annual charge after the trial ("trap", July 2026), with refunds offered. — [Trustpilot getinflow.io](https://at.trustpilot.com/review/getinflow.io)
- EndeavorOTC: App Store ratings vary by source (about 3.9–4.0 from about 1,600 ratings; another aggregator shows 2.8). Google Play shows 3.4 from 404 reviews. Complaints cover high subscription cost, short free trial, motion sickness and lag from tilt controls, and little game variety. — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6447322997); [apptail](https://apptail.io/app/endeavorotc-outplay-adhd-gcIm/united-states); [asolytics](https://free.asolytics.pro/app-store/akili-interactive-labs-inc/endeavorotc)
- In the VR study, preferences between human and AI doubles were split. Some wanted a human for motivation and competitiveness; others preferred AI to avoid feeling judged. Humans were preferred for studying, AI was seen as enough for repetitive work. — [Ara et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1)

### Inferences
- In a family app, the companion should never need a camera. Default to "present" signals (a ping, a live "Mum is with you" indicator, an optional voice or video call). Keep the end check-in non-judgemental (celebrate any progress, as Flow Club does).
- Family members can carry more judgement risk than strangers for someone with rejection sensitivity. The companion's role should be framed as company, not supervision.

### Gaps
- Direct Reddit, App Store and ADDitude pages for Shimmer, Dubbii and Flow Club were not fetched. I have no reliable user-sentiment summary for those three.

## 5. Pricing (late 2026 where available)

### Takeaway
Prices range from free (Focusmate basic, Dubbii freemium) through about $8–40/month (body doubling and self-guided apps) to about $230–345/month (human 1:1 coaching). Third-party figures often conflict.

### Cited Findings
- **Focusmate:** free for 3 sessions a week; Plus (unlimited) $8/mo billed yearly or $12/mo billed monthly. — [Focusmate FAQ, fetched Oct 2026](https://www.focusmate.com/faq). Some third-party sites list $6.99/$10.99 instead. — [lifestack.ai](https://lifestack.ai/blog/focusmate-pricing)

## 6. What a family PWA could rebuild cheaply vs what needs a human service

### Takeaway
Most of the body-doubling and accountability loop can be rebuilt cheaply: a scheduled start, a stated goal, a "join me?" ping, a visible presence timer, an end check-in, and gentle streaks. What cannot be rebuilt without a human service is reliable stranger matching at any hour, trained coaching judgement, and clinical-grade CBT content with evidence behind it. EndeavorOTC's mechanic should not be copied, because it targets lab-measured attention, not starting tasks.

### Cited Findings (mechanics to copy, each tied to its source)
- **Session with a stated goal and an end check** (25/50/75 min; say the task, work, report at the end). — [Focusmate FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq); [Flow Club blog](https://www.flow.club/blog/body-doubling-session-format)
- **Short sessions with fewer people keep check-ins fast** (Flow Club cut 30-minute groups to 5 people because check-ins took too long). — [Flow Club blog](https://www.flow.club/blog/body-doubling-session-format)
- **Calendar invite for every booked session.** — [Focusmate FAQ](https://www.focusmate.com/faq)
- **Fallback when the partner is missing** (rematch, or next slot in 15 min). — [Focusmate help](https://support.focusmate.com/en/articles/4044431-what-if-i-don-t-get-a-match-or-my-partner-doesn-t-show)
- **Non-verbal, camera-free mode.** — [Flow Club blog](https://www.flow.club/blog/body-doubling-session-format); [Reddit snapshots](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1scn98w/snapshots/2026-04-10T20%3A30%3A07.765544Z)
- **Recorded companion videos and micro-steps for household tasks** (Dubbii). — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6450302677)
- **Weekly plan → small steps → mid-week text check-in → weekly reflection** (Shimmer coaching cycle). — [shimmer.care](https://www.shimmer.care/)
- **If-then plans** (experimental support in children with ADHD). — [Gawrilow et al.](https://www.socmot.uni-konstanz.de/sites/default/files/Gawrilow_Gollwitzer_Oettingen_Delay_2011-2.pdf)
- **Human and simulated presence gave similar short-term effects** in a 12-person VR study. — [Ara et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12153v1)

### Inferences
**Cheap to rebuild in a family PWA**
1. **"Starting now, join me?" ping** to a chosen family member or friend. The starter picks the task and length (10/25/50 min). The ping carries the stated goal (only what the user chooses to share). The family member taps "I'm with you," and a shared presence indicator shows on both phones. No camera is needed. Optional link to a FaceTime or voice call for those who want it.
2. **Scheduled sessions** that a family member books for the user ("Tue 10:00, paperwork, with Dad"). The user receives a calendar file (.ics) and a push notification, which addresses forgetting to open the app. The companion's own reminder helps because the companion will look for the user.
3. **Session screen:** large task text, a visible timer, an "I'm stuck" button that offers a smaller first step, and an end screen ("Done / partly / didn't start, that's OK") that can be shared with the companion as a tiny celebration.
4. **Fallback companion** when no human is free: a pre-recorded clip from a family member, or a simulated "virtual session" clearly labelled as simulated (per project rules). The Ara et al. human ≈ AI finding is weak support for this.
5. **If-then cue setup** ("When I [anchor], I start [task] for 10 min") attached to scheduled sessions.
6. **Gentle accountability instead of penalties:** a "showed up" count visible to the companion, with no freezes or scores. Streaks and penalty scores risk shame for users with rejection sensitivity.
7. **Weekly mini-review** modelled on Shimmer's cycle (what worked, one strategy to try next week). A family member could take part in the review.

**Needs a human service (or is out of scope)**
- Matching with strangers 24/7 (Focusmate's "99% matched" depends on a large user base). A family app has a small pool, so plan for "nobody available."
- Trained coaching judgement (Shimmer's vetted coaches, assessments, tailored strategies). Family members are not coaches and can add relationship strain.
- Structured CBT curriculum with outcome evidence (Inflow). The app could link to it, but rebuilding validated CBT content is costly, and claims would need evidence.
- FDA-cleared attention training (EndeavorOTC). It is regulated, targets lab attention, and does not address starting or remembering tasks.
- Crisis handling: the Inflow RCT found about 8.5% of screened adults with ADHD reported suicidal thoughts. Any app with reflection features should at least surface crisis resources. — [Simply Psychology summary](https://www.simplypsychology.org/?p=97474)

### Gaps
- No study tests family members specifically as body doubles. Eagle et al. report who the body doubles were, but the breakdown was not retrieved.
- iOS PWA limits on push notifications, background timers and calendar integration are outside this note's scope. They are covered in the project's `docs/PWA_PREP.md` and possibly by other researchers.
