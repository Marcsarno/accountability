# ADHD visual planner, timer and routine apps: how they get adults to START tasks and stay on them

Research date: 2026-10-28. Apps covered: Tiimo, Structured, Routinery, Llama Life, Focus Bear, Brili Routines, Sunsama, Motion. Most mechanics come from App Store listings (fetched late October 2026, with version notes dated Sep–Oct 2026) and vendor pages. Independent ADHD user voice was harder to find. Reddit threads did not come up in the searches (see Gaps), so user sentiment mostly comes from App Store reviews and blog reviews. Older items carry their dates.

## 1. Core mechanic for getting started (per app, with daily flow where documented)

### Takeaway
The apps fall into three families. (a) **Visual timeline / day planners** (Tiimo, Structured) put each task on a colour- and icon-coded timeline with a "now" line and a focus countdown. (b) **Step-by-step routine players** (Routinery, Brili, Focus Bear routines) run a saved routine one step at a time, with a timer for each step and voice or sound cues for the next step. (c) **Single-task countdown lists** (Llama Life) show one task with its own countdown, plus a chime and an "extend or done" choice. Sunsama (guided planning ritual) and Motion (AI auto-scheduling) are knowledge-worker tools that some sources recommend for ADHD because they remove the "what next?" decision. For the "can't start basic tasks" problem, the routine-player and single-task patterns are the most directly relevant.

### Cited Findings

**Tiimo (visual timeline + focus timer + AI co-planner)**
- Features listed by the developer: AI Co-Planner ("create, prioritize, and plan tasks"), a colour-coded drag-and-drop Visual Schedule/timeline, a brain-dump To-Do list with AI priority grouping, a countdown Focus Timer, Apple Calendar/Reminders/Google Calendar sync, checklists/subtasks on task cards, and **timed subtasks** (v3.69.0, Oct 12 2026) — [Tiimo App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328)
- Recent additions: "snap-a-photo-to-tasks" and Siri/App Intents (3.69.0), editable and selectable AI-suggested tasks with "smoother onboarding" (3.69.1, Oct 13 2026), and Apple Watch Focus sessions (3.68.0/3.70.x, Oct 2026) — [Tiimo App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328)
- The vendor describes the Focus timer as a way to "make time visible" and to stay anchored in tasks while managing transitions — [Tiimo FAQ](https://www.tiimoapp.com/faq)

**Structured (visual timeline)**
- Tasks and calendar events share one vertical timeline. Each item has a start time, duration, colour and icon, and drag-and-drop reschedules it. A "now" line marks the current time. There is an Inbox for tasks without a time, plus subtasks and notes for breaking a task into a concrete first step (Oct 5 2026 review) — [adhdproductivity.org](https://adhdproductivity.org/structured-review-for-adhd/)
- Free: timeline, inbox, Pomodoro focus timer, Live Activities, interactive widgets. Pro: auto-replanning of missed tasks, calendar/Reminders import, recurring tasks/routines, AI day drafting, custom notifications, focus "Intervals" (v4.5.0, Jun 3 2026). Version 4.6.0 (Sep 11 2026) added "Smart Task Icons", a widget setup guide and natural-language task creation (beta). Version 4.6.4 (Sep 29 2026) added Siri AI task creation and "Suggested Subtasks" — [Structured App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner-todo/id1499198946)
- Energy Monitor (free) shows how demanding the scheduled day looks — [adhdproductivity.org](https://adhdproductivity.org/structured-review-for-adhd/). An unofficial open-source reconstruction describes it as Recharging (+1), Neutral (0) and Draining (-1) per activity. It is not official, so treat with caution — [GitHub reconstruction](https://github.com/hariharan-uiux/structured-planner)

**Routinery (step-by-step routine player)**
- A routine is broken into 1–5-minute steps, each with its own timer set once when you build the routine. You start the routine and "a voice calls out what comes next", reading each step aloud so the routine keeps running while you shower or cook. Mid-run you can "stretch a timer, skip a step, reorder what's left". It also has Pomodoro blocks, standalone one-off tasks (3.30.x, Sep–Oct 2026), a Timeline view and an AI-assistant connection via MCP (3.29.34, Aug 2026) — [Routinery App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-reminder-routine-timer/id1450486923)
- One ADHD comparison guide says Routinery covers executing a routine you already have, not planning a full day — [getinflow.io](https://www.getinflow.io/post/best-alternatives-routinery-adhd) (via search summary)

**Llama Life (single-task countdown list)**
- Each task in a list gets its own countdown. The aim is to give that one task full attention "until the timer runs out". The app shows total list time and an **estimated finish time**, and reports planned vs actual time (time spent is editable). Tasks reorder by drag. A "Not-Now" list holds deferred tasks. Preset task lists cover recurring routines. Confetti plays on completion. There is Todoist integration — [Llama Life App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adhd-organizer-llama-life/id6454469750)
- When a timer ends, a "gentle chime" sounds and you choose to extend the task or mark it done. The app nudges single-tasking: new ideas go into the list instead of being acted on. White noise plays during work (Aug 5 2024 review, published by competitor Focus Bear) — [Focus Bear blog](https://www.focusbear.io/blog-post/review-of-llama-life-by-an-audhder)

**Focus Bear (routine + blocking)**
- Morning and evening routines guide you "habit by habit", scheduled or on demand. It blocks distracting apps and sites during focus sessions and routines, with "soft blocking" (a pause, not a wall) and per-habit app allowances (1.18.0, Apr 2026). Shortcuts start a focus session or "the next habit". It also has a smart to-do list with voice/screenshot capture, movement and break reminders, and streaks with completion animations — [Focus Bear App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focus-bear-built-for-audhders/id1673296334)

**Brili Routines (adult version; routine "playlists")**
- Routines are "well-paced playlists" with on-demand routines, templates and a "unique visual clock". The app automatically calculates the time left to finish all tasks in a routine. Customisable rewards are redeemed in a "Celebrate" tab — [Brili App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1516036620)
- An App Store reviewer says the adult version lets you skip or remove tasks from an active routine, which was easier than the kids' app (via search summary) — [Brili App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1516036620)

**Sunsama (guided daily planning ritual)**
- A guided morning ritual: review yesterday, pull in tasks from integrations, estimate each task, drag them onto the calendar, and set a realistic workload. There is also a shutdown ritual — [temporal.day](https://temporal.day/blog/sunsama-vs-motion)
- Reviewers say the ritual asks about yesterday's work, today's goals, time estimates and expected challenges. They call it the most praised feature because it eases anxiety about what to do first — [toolfinder.com](https://toolfinder.com/comparisons/motion-vs-sunsama) (via search summary)

**Motion (AI auto-scheduling)**
- You add a task with a deadline and priority, and Motion's algorithm picks the calendar slot. When a meeting is added or a deadline shifts, it reschedules automatically — [temporal.day](https://temporal.day/blog/sunsama-vs-motion)
- It is often recommended for ADHD "because it removes the decision of what to do next" — [temporal.day](https://temporal.day/blog/sunsama-vs-motion)

### Inferences
- For one adult who cannot start *basic* tasks, the strongest "start" mechanic is the **routine player**: a saved list, one "Start" button, then the app drives each step by voice or sound and a per-step timer. The user never re-decides what comes next. Next strongest is the **single-task countdown** (Llama Life): one task on screen, a countdown, a chime, and extend-or-done.
- The other common pattern is a **tiny first step**: timed subtasks (Tiimo 3.69.0), Suggested Subtasks (Structured 4.6.4) and 1–5-minute steps (Routinery). All three make the first action small and concrete.
- Auto-scheduling (Motion) and planning rituals (Sunsama) assume the user opens a desktop app to plan. That does not fit someone who forgets to open apps.

### Gaps
- No source gave a full screen-by-screen walkthrough of Tiimo's or Structured's daily "now" flow, for example exactly what the screen shows when a task starts. Descriptions are feature-level.
- What Routinery does exactly when a step's timer runs out (auto-advance or overtime) is not documented on the listing.
- Brili's behaviour when running late is not documented.

## 2. How each app reaches the user who forgets (notifications, widgets, Lock Screen, Live Activities, Watch, Focus)

### Takeaway
Every leading app leans heavily on **native iOS surfaces**: Live Activities / Dynamic Island countdowns (Tiimo, Structured, Llama Life), home and Lock Screen widgets (Tiimo, Structured, Routinery, Focus Bear) and Apple Watch (Tiimo, Structured, Routinery, Focus Bear). Notification patterns are simple: start and end reminders, with sounds and vibration. Routinery adds voice callouts and Focus Bear adds escalating "visual then spoken" reminders. Alarm fatigue is a recognised failure mode. A web app can reproduce only part of this: Web Push in an installed iOS PWA, plus in-app sound and voice while the page is open.

### Cited Findings
- **Tiimo:** the free plan includes reminders with notifications, vibration and sounds. The listing describes "ongoing reminders halfway through and at the end of activities" to help with transitions — [Tiimo App Store (via search summary)](https://apps.apple.com/app/tiimo/id1480220328). Settings sit under Settings > Notifications, where you toggle the reminder types you want — [Tiimo help (search snippet)](https://help.tiimoapp.com/ios-features-how-tos/notification-reminders-and-custom-sounds). The help page now redirects to the FAQ, which only says notifications help "without creating alert fatigue" and that sounds need the phone off silent — [Tiimo FAQ](https://www.tiimoapp.com/faq)
- **Tiimo:** one user sets reminders for the beginning and end of each activity and gets "a buzz on my wrist" when focus runs long — [ADHD Open Space Substack](https://adhdos.substack.com/p/how-tiimo-can-help-you-manage-your)
- **Tiimo:** an ADHD reviewer (2021) said the notification "ding" was too brief to pull attention back to the next task. The developer said a more persistent alarm was being considered — [Tiimo App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328). The feedback board has an open request for distinct sounds for event start vs reminder — [tiimo.nolt.io](https://tiimo.nolt.io/18)
- **Tiimo Live Activities / widgets:** timers and task progress stay visible in real time on the Lock Screen and in Dynamic Island while other apps are in use (example: "Get your toughest work done", 42 min left). The Focus Timer widget shows a visual countdown, with "no need to open the app" — [Tiimo widgets & Live Activities](https://tiimoapp.com/product/widgets-live-activities). XL widgets came in 3.67.0, Live Activity timers with custom task icons in 3.68.0, and Watch complications in 3.68.0/3.69.0 — [Tiimo App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328)
- **Structured:** Live Activities and interactive widgets are in the free tier. Live Activities now "dismiss promptly after completing a task". Version 4.4.8 (May 7 2026) added a separate **morning planning reminder** and an **overdue reminder**. Custom notifications are Pro. It syncs to Apple Watch and Mac — [Structured App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner-todo/id1499198946). A reviewer recommends limiting alerts to start-of-task reminders for anchor tasks plus perhaps one end-of-day reminder — [adhdproductivity.org](https://adhdproductivity.org/structured-review-for-adhd/)
- **Routinery:** scheduled routines notify at a chosen weekday and time. 3.30.3 (Oct 15 2026) lets you choose when time-of-day reminders arrive. During a run, a voice reads each step. A home screen widget shows today's list, and Lock Screen widgets for routine progress and starting a routine came in 3.29.33 (Aug 13 2026). You can continue a routine on Watch, iPad or Mac. The listing does not mention Live Activities — [Routinery App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-reminder-routine-timer/id1450486923)
- **Routinery reliability:** the developer acknowledged a notification bug and a report of routines disappearing — [Routinery App Store (via search summary)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-reminder-routine-timer/id1450486923). A comparison guide reports user complaints of bugs after updates affecting timer accuracy and routine transitions — [getinflow.io](https://www.getinflow.io/post/best-alternatives-routinery-adhd)
- **Llama Life:** Live Activities and Dynamic Island came in 2.15.127 (Nov 26 2024). Alarms that play for up to 30 seconds when the app is closed were improved in 2.31.151 (Oct 9, year not shown, likely 2026). The listing shows no widgets or Watch app — [Llama Life App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adhd-organizer-llama-life/id6454469750)
- **Focus Bear:** the "Late No More" feature sends escalating meeting reminders, first visual, then **spoken**. 1.24.1 (Oct 6 2026) added a **persistent focus-timer notification**. It has Focus, Streak and Blocked Message widgets (1.20.0, Jun 2026) and runs on Apple Watch — [Focus Bear App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focus-bear-built-for-audhders/id1673296334)
- **Brili:** "Consistent notifications nudge you to stay on track", with acoustic and visual alerts — [Brili App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1516036620)
- **General:** a reminder-app guide names "alarm fatigue", where a wall of daily notifications trains you to ignore all of them, as a common reason reminders fail — [Braindump blog](https://braindumpnotes.app/blog/best-adhd-reminder-apps)

### Inferences
- **Mechanics that need native iOS** (not available to a PWA): Live Activities and Dynamic Island countdowns, home and Lock Screen widgets, Apple Watch apps and complications, Siri/App Intents, Screen Time-based app blocking (Focus Bear), alarms that ring for 30 s with the app closed (Llama Life), and Focus-mode filters.
- **Mechanics a web app can rebuild:** start, halfway and end reminders sent via Web Push (needs a server to send at the right time), a "morning planning" push and an "overdue" push (Structured 4.4.8), voice callouts with `speechSynthesis` while the page is open (Routinery), escalating reminders (repeat pushes, then a spoken cue in-app; Focus Bear), and chimes with an extend-or-done prompt (Llama Life).
- A PWA cannot keep a countdown on the Lock Screen. The closest substitutes are push notifications at fixed points (for example start, halfway and two minutes left) and the app badge (Badging API, where supported). This needs checking against `docs/PWA_PREP.md` in the project.
- Since the user "forgets to even open apps", the push channel and its reliability matter more than any in-app visual. Keep it to a few pushes that matter, to avoid alarm fatigue.

### Gaps
- Tiimo's exact reminder offsets (for example 5 or 10 minutes before start) and whether a halfway alert is on by default could not be verified. The help page now redirects.
- Structured's notification offsets and default alert times are not documented in the sources found.
- No source described any of these apps re-alerting (repeat or nag) when a start notification is ignored, apart from Focus Bear's escalation for meetings.

## 3. How they make time visible (time blindness)

### Takeaway
The recurring devices are: a **"now" line on a vertical timeline** with blocks sized by duration (Structured, Tiimo), a **live countdown** (rings or bars) for the current task, mirrored to the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, a **"finish at" / total-time-left** figure for the whole list or routine (Llama Life, Brili), and **planned vs actual** reports (Llama Life). Halfway and end alerts (Tiimo) and spoken step changes (Routinery) turn time into sound for when the user isn't looking.

### Cited Findings
- Structured: blocks sized by duration plus a current-time line show how much of the day is left. A full timeline makes overcommitment obvious. The reviewer suggests grey colour for transition and buffer blocks — [adhdproductivity.org](https://adhdproductivity.org/structured-review-for-adhd/)
- Tiimo: the focus timer is meant to "make time visible" — [Tiimo FAQ](https://www.tiimoapp.com/faq). The Live Activity shows minutes left on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island — [Tiimo widgets page](https://tiimoapp.com/product/widgets-live-activities). Halfway and end reminders support transitions — [Tiimo App Store (via search summary)](https://apps.apple.com/app/tiimo/id1480220328)
- Llama Life: total list time, estimated finish time, and planned vs actual time in reports — [Llama Life App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adhd-organizer-llama-life/id6454469750). The chime pulls the user out of hyperfocus (reviewer's account of time blindness) — [Focus Bear blog, Aug 2024](https://www.focusbear.io/blog-post/review-of-llama-life-by-an-audhder)
- Brili: a visual clock that "automatically calculates the time left to complete all of your tasks" — [Brili App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1516036620)
- Routinery: an App Store reviewer said it helped with time blindness and staying on one task (06/17/2024) — [Routinery App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-reminder-routine-timer/id1450486923)
- Sunsama: the user estimates each task during the ritual, and the app warns when planned work exceeds a healthy limit — [temporal.day](https://temporal.day/blog/sunsama-vs-motion)

### Inferences
- These are cheap to rebuild in a web app: a big countdown ring, an "at this pace you finish at 08:42" line for a routine, and logging of estimated vs actual minutes. The Lock Screen mirror is the part a PWA cannot reproduce.
- Planned-vs-actual data could later improve default step durations. This is speculative; no app documented doing this automatically.

### Gaps
- No source quantified how far ADHD users' estimates miss, or showed whether showing estimate vs actual improves future estimates.

## 4. What happens when the user misses or falls behind

### Takeaway
The pattern is **forgiving, not punishing**. Structured (Pro) auto-replans missed tasks and avoids red overdue badges. Sunsama carries unfinished tasks forward. Llama Life offers "extend" at time-up and a Not-Now list. Routinery and Brili let you skip, stretch or reorder steps mid-run. Motion reshuffles automatically, which reviewers say can feel overwhelming or controlling. Streaks (Focus Bear) are the only mildly punitive mechanic found.

### Cited Findings
- Structured: "Replanning" (Pro) automatically reschedules missed or forgotten tasks. There is also an "overdue reminder" (4.4.8) — [Structured App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner-todo/id1499198946). There are no red overdue badges, and the reviewer drags unfinished blocks down "rather than feel guilty". Replan is iOS-only — [adhdproductivity.org](https://adhdproductivity.org/structured-review-for-adhd/)
- Structured user request: unexpected tasks should push later items automatically, and some events should be "locked" so they don't move (developer pointed to roadmap) — [Structured App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner-todo/id1499198946)
- Routinery: "stretch a timer, skip a step, reorder what's left" without leaving the run — [Routinery App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-reminder-routine-timer/id1450486923)
- Llama Life: at time-up you extend or mark done. A Not-Now list holds deferred tasks — [Focus Bear blog](https://www.focusbear.io/blog-post/review-of-llama-life-by-an-audhder); [Llama Life App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adhd-organizer-llama-life/id6454469750). The reviewer calls the coaching gentler than apps that feel like a "drill sergeant", and says the mascot makes them not want to "let the llama down" — [Focus Bear blog](https://www.focusbear.io/blog-post/review-of-llama-life-by-an-audhder)
- Brili: you can skip or remove tasks from an active routine (adult version). A critical review asked the app to stop routines overlapping and to "automatically adjust times to avoid conflicts" (June 2023) — [Brili App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1516036620)
- Sunsama: an ADHD-focused list highlights automatic carry-over of unfinished tasks — [lifestack.ai](https://lifestack.ai/blog/adhd-daily-planner) (via search summary). A workload limit prevents overload — [temporal.day](https://temporal.day/blog/sunsama-vs-motion)
- Motion: reschedules everything when plans shift. For some users, "constant reshuffling can feel like a loss of control" — [toolfinder.com](https://toolfinder.com/comparisons/motion-vs-sunsama) (via search summary). Its "aggressive rescheduling can also cause overwhelm" — [temporal.day](https://temporal.day/blog/sunsama-vs-motion)
- Focus Bear: streaks and completion animations — [Focus Bear App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focus-bear-built-for-audhders/id1673296334)

### Inferences
- Rebuild **one-tap recovery**: "Missed — do it now / move to later / drop". Do not show red overdue states, and do not shuffle the whole plan silently. Motion's experience suggests any auto-move should be visible and approved by the user.
- Skip, stretch and "+5 min" controls during a routine are basic expectations.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence on whether streaks help or hurt adults with ADHD in these specific apps.

## 5. Setup and onboarding burden

### Takeaway
The timeline planners need real up-front building (colours, anchors, buffers, routines). Users call it tedious but worth it. Vendors are now cutting this with AI: AI co-planner and suggested tasks (Tiimo), natural-language, photo and Siri task creation (Structured), and templates (Routinery, Brili). Routine players need each routine built once (steps plus durations), then reuse it daily.

### Cited Findings
- Structured: about 15 minutes of setup (a few colours, fixed anchors, buffers, an inbox rule). The reviewer warns that minute-level over-planning "killed his early use" — [adhdproductivity.org](https://adhdproductivity.org/structured-review-for-adhd/)
- Tiimo: a blogger says setup is tedious but necessary to get the benefits — [ADHD Open Space Substack (via search summary)](https://adhdos.substack.com/p/how-tiimo-can-help-you-manage-your). AI-suggested tasks and "smoother onboarding" arrived in 3.69.1 (Oct 13 2026). Snap-a-photo-to-tasks arrived in 3.69.0 — [Tiimo App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328)
- Routinery: step lengths are set once when you build a routine. The free tier allows 2 routines with no account — [Routinery App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-reminder-routine-timer/id1450486923)
- Brili: pre-made templates and a routine creator. A reviewer found routine management clumsy when routines overlap — [Brili App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1516036620)
- Focus Bear: an AuDHD test during onboarding (1.24.1). Block lists are configurable — [Focus Bear App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focus-bear-built-for-audhders/id1673296334)
- Motion: reviewers say the AI takes a week or two to learn your preferences — [toolfinder.com](https://toolfinder.com/comparisons/motion-vs-sunsama) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- For a single user, ship pre-filled routines (for example "get up", "leave the house", "evening reset") with sensible 1–5-minute steps the user can edit. Do not start from a blank timeline.

### Gaps
- No measured onboarding times or drop-off data from any vendor.

## 6. What ADHD users praise and complain about

### Takeaway
The praise centres on visual calm, step-by-step guidance, timers that pull users out of hyperfocus, and gentle tone. The complaints centre on alerts too weak to break attention (Tiimo, 2021), reliability bugs in timers and notifications (Routinery, Llama Life), features behind paywalls (Structured), billing friction (Llama Life) and setup effort.

### Cited Findings
- Structured: "finally a Planner I won't forget about". Another reviewer says the layout "was designed for a low-dopamine brain". Rated 4.8 from about 168K ratings — [Structured App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structured-daily-planner-todo/id1499198946). Cons from the ADHD review: one-way calendar sync, key features Pro-only, web version in beta — [adhdproductivity.org](https://adhdproductivity.org/structured-review-for-adhd/)
- Tiimo: rated 4.6 from about 21K ratings. Complaints (2021): the notification ding was too short, and the Watch logged the user out mid-activity — [Tiimo App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328)
- Routinery: a late-diagnosed ADHD/ASD reviewer (01/08/2023) called it "SO HELPFUL", praising per-task timers, voice alerts and quick reordering. Rated 4.7 from about 18K ratings — [Routinery App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-reminder-routine-timer/id1450486923). Reports of bugs in timer accuracy and transitions after updates — [getinflow.io](https://www.getinflow.io/post/best-alternatives-routinery-adhd)
- Llama Life: "Simple, relaxing, takes away stress instead of replacing it". Rated 4.5 from 117 ratings. Version 2.31.151 fixed task lists reverting to older versions — [Llama Life App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/adhd-organizer-llama-life/id6454469750). Third-party summaries report billing complaints (unclear free vs paid tier, cancellation) — [toolfinder.co / search summary](https://toolfinder.co/tools/llama-life)
- Focus Bear: "stopped so many nights of doomscrolling", and "so much nicer than Tiimo". A complaint that the timer resets to its original setting. Only 13 ratings — [Focus Bear App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focus-bear-built-for-audhders/id1673296334). Some users say cost is a barrier after the trial — [toolradar (via search summary)](https://toolradar.com/tools/focus-bear)
- Brili: "incredibly helpful with automating my mornings" (Feb 2022), and "a $50/year app but it feels… like they're still beta testing" (June 2024). Rated 4.6 from 603 ratings. The latest version is 2.38 (Nov 25 2025), so updates appear slow — [Brili App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1516036620)
- Sunsama/Motion: ADHD-specific points come mainly from affiliate-style comparison sites. Sunsama's ritual "gives structure without surprises", and Motion removes the next-task decision but can overwhelm — [temporal.day](https://temporal.day/blog/sunsama-vs-motion). Note that Temporal is a competitor.

### Inferences
- The most important lesson for the PWA: the start cue must be **strong enough to break attention** (sound plus vibration plus possibly voice), and timers and notifications must be **reliable**. Broken timers destroy trust quickly.

### Gaps
- **Reddit threads (r/ADHD, r/adhdwomen, r/ADHD_Programmers, r/productivity) did not come up in the searches**, including a `site:reddit.com` search. No YouTube or ADDitude/CHADD reviews were retrieved. User voice here is limited to App Store reviews and blogs, and App Store samples are small and skew positive.

## 7. Pricing (2026) and evidence claims / awards

### Takeaway
Prices sit in the $3–$8/month or $30–$80/year range for the ADHD-specific apps. Sunsama and Motion cost about $19–$25/month. Awards and recognition (Tiimo iPhone App of the Year 2025, Apple Design Award finalist 2024, Editors' Choice for several) are design accolades, not clinical evidence. **No app cited a peer-reviewed study of its own efficacy.** Routinery explicitly says it is not a medical device.

### Cited Findings
- **Tiimo:** free tier (basic planner, limited AI chats, 1 profile). Pro includes the web app. A 7-day trial applies only to the yearly plan. In-app purchase prices are listed from $7 to $54, without labels — [Tiimo App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiimo-to-do-list-planner/id1480220328); [Tiimo FAQ](https://www.tiimoapp.com/faq). ### Inferences
- The evidence base for these apps is design awards and testimonials, not trials. Mechanics should be chosen by plausibility and user feedback, not efficacy claims.

### Gaps
- No clinical advisor names or published studies were found for any app in this search. CHADD and ADDitude coverage was not retrieved.
- Several prices conflict between the App Store and third parties (Tiimo, Focus Bear). Regional and in-app checkout prices may differ.
